Stewardship

Support the Mission

Your generosity supports worship, evangelization, parish activities and the ongoing work of Sacred Heart Cathedral. Every contribution advances the work of the Gospel in the Archdiocese of Calabar.

Why We Give

Giving as an Act of Faith

Christian stewardship is the grateful response of a disciple to the call of Christ. Everything we have is a gift from God — our time, our talents and our treasure. Giving back to God through His Church is an act of worship, trust and love.

At Sacred Heart Cathedral, your generosity makes possible the daily celebration of the Holy Sacraments, the upkeep of our sacred buildings, the formation of our children in the faith, and outreach to the poor and needy in our community.

“Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.” — 2 Corinthians 9:7

Christian Stewardship

Where Your Gift Goes

Every contribution is used responsibly for the mission of the Church. Here are just some of the ways your giving makes a difference.

The Work of the Gospel

Your generous giving sustains the pastoral, liturgical, charitable and formational work of Sacred Heart Cathedral. It is an investment in the Kingdom of God.

  • Daily Mass & Liturgy — Supporting the priests and the sacred liturgy offered daily for the people of God.
  • Catechism & Formation — Forming children and adults in the Catholic faith.
  • Charity to the Poor — Supporting the Society of St. Vincent de Paul and outreach to those in need.
  • Cathedral Upkeep — Maintaining our sacred heritage for future generations.
  • Evangelization — Spreading the Gospel in Calabar and beyond through media and outreach.
Thank You

Your Generosity Changes Lives

We are deeply grateful for every gift, no matter the size. Your generosity makes the mission of Sacred Heart Cathedral possible. May God reward you abundantly.
