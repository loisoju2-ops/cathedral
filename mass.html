Sacraments & Formation

Grow in Faith & Grace

The seven sacraments are the wellspring of grace in the Christian life. Learn how to receive them at Sacred Heart Cathedral.

Confession

The sacrament of Reconciliation is where we encounter the boundless mercy of God and receive absolution for our sins.

  • Saturdays immediately after the 6:00 AM Mass
  • At the Shrine
  • Also available by appointment with any priest
Learn More →

Baptism

Baptism is the gateway to life in the Spirit and the door which gives access to the other sacraments.

  • Infant Baptism: Every 1st Saturday of the month during the 6:00 AM Mass
  • Parents and godparents must attend preparation class
  • Adult Baptism: Contact the parish office
Learn More →

Confirmation

Confirmation perfects Baptismal grace and strengthens the confirmed with the gifts of the Holy Spirit.

  • Registration opens annually
  • Candidate must be baptized and receive First Holy Communion
  • Attendance at catechism classes required
Learn More →

First Holy Communion

The Eucharist is the source and summit of the Christian life. Preparation for children is a joyful milestone.

  • Children's Catechism: Saturdays at 4:00 PM
  • Candidate must be baptized
  • Parents are expected to attend preparation sessions
Learn More →

Matrimony

Holy Matrimony is a covenant by which a man and a woman establish between themselves a partnership of the whole of life.

  • Marriage preparation classes: Saturdays at 5:00 PM
  • Couples should contact the parish office at least 6 months in advance
  • Requirements: Baptismal certificates, banns, pre-marital counselling
Learn More →

Formation & Teaching

Catechism

Growing in knowledge and love of the faith through systematic formation for children and adults.

Classes & Formation

Catechism classes at Sacred Heart Cathedral provide a firm foundation in the Catholic faith for children and adults alike. Our dedicated catechists guide learners through the truths of the faith, the sacraments, prayer and Christian living.

  • Children's Catechism — Saturdays at 4:00 PM. Preparation for First Holy Communion and Confirmation.
  • Adult Catechism — For adults seeking Baptism, Confirmation or a deeper understanding of the faith.
  • Marriage Preparation — Saturdays at 5:00 PM for couples preparing for Holy Matrimony.
  • Baptism Preparation — For parents and godparents before infant baptism.
