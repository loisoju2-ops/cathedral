Ministries & Groups

There is a place for everyone at Sacred Heart Cathedral. Discover our lay movements, pious societies, and liturgical and charitable groups — and find where you belong.

A Place for Everyone

Serve God, Serve His People

The ministries and societies of Sacred Heart Cathedral are the beating heart of our parish life. Through them, the faithful are formed in faith, grow in holiness, and put their gifts at the service of God and neighbour.

Whether you are called to liturgical service at the altar, to prayer and devotion, to charitable works, or to the formation of young people, there is a place for you here. We invite you to prayerfully consider joining one of the groups below.

“As each one has received a gift, use it to serve one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace.” — 1 Peter 4:10

Lay Movements

The organised lay faithful of the parish, serving the Church and society.

CMO

Catholic Men's Organisation

Bringing together the men of the parish for fellowship, service and the promotion of Christian family life.

CWO

Catholic Women's Organisation

Uniting the women of the parish in prayer, service and the building up of the Christian home.

CYON

Catholic Youth Organisation of Nigeria

Empowering young Catholics to live their faith boldly through fellowship, formation and outreach.

MOD

Missionary Organisation of the Deaf

Serving the deaf community in the parish, sharing the Gospel through sign language and fellowship.

Pious Societies

Devotional societies dedicated to prayer, the saints and the Blessed Virgin Mary.

SHJ

Sacred Heart of Jesus

A society devoted to the love and mercy of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, promoting devotion and reparation.

IHM

Immaculate Heart of Mary

Honouring the Immaculate Heart of Mary through prayer, consecration and the imitation of her virtues.

LoM

Legion of Mary

A lay apostolic movement serving the parish and community under the banner of Our Lady.

CCRN

Catholic Charismatic Renewal

Renewing the faithful in the power of the Holy Spirit through prayer, praise and the gifts of the Spirit.

BRC

Block Rosary Crusade

Bringing families together to pray the Holy Rosary in their neighbourhoods, fostering faith and fellowship.

WAF

World Apostolate of Fatima

Promoting the message of Our Lady of Fatima and the practice of the First Saturday devotion.

StJ

St. Jude Society

A society dedicated to St. Jude Thaddeus, the patron of impossible causes, offering prayer and support.

PS

Purgatorian Society

Praying for the faithful departed and offering support to the bereaved in their time of loss.

HFG

Holy Family Guild

Promoting the ideals of the Holy Family of Nazareth and supporting Christian family life in the parish.

Liturgical Groups

Serving at the altar and in the sacred liturgy of the Church.

AS

Altar Servers

Young men and boys assisting the priest at the altar during Mass and other liturgical celebrations.

ML

Mary League

Young girls dedicated to serving at the altar under the patronage of the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Charitable Groups

Serving the poor, the sick and the vulnerable in the name of Christ.

SVP

Society of St. Vincent de Paul

Offering practical help, food, clothing and companionship to those in need in our community.

Get Involved

Your Gifts Are Needed

Every member of the parish has been given gifts by the Holy Spirit for the building up of the Body of Christ. The ministries and societies of Sacred Heart Cathedral are waiting for you to bring your talents, your time and your heart.

Whether you can serve at the altar, lead a rosary, visit the sick, or simply offer a welcoming smile, there is a place for you.
