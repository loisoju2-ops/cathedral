Who We Are

A Sacred Home of Faith, Worship & Community

Sacred Heart Cathedral is the mother church of the Catholic Archdiocese of Calabar. For generations, it has stood as a beacon of faith in the heart of Cross River State — a place where the faithful gather to celebrate the Holy Sacraments, to pray, and to encounter the living God.

As the seat of the Metropolitan Archbishop, the Cathedral is the spiritual centre of the Archdiocese. It is here that the local Church gathers for its most solemn liturgies, and from here that the Gospel is proclaimed throughout the length and breadth of the Archdiocese.

Whether you are a lifelong parishioner or a first-time visitor, you are welcome here. Sacred Heart Cathedral is a home for all who seek the Lord.

Metropolitan Archbishop

His Grace, Most Rev. Joseph Effiong Ekuwem

His Grace, Most Rev. Joseph Effiong Ekuwem, serves as the Metropolitan Archbishop of the Catholic Archdiocese of Calabar. As the chief shepherd of the Archdiocese, he is the spiritual father of all the faithful in Calabar and its environs.

Under his leadership, the Archdiocese continues to grow in faith, evangelization and service to the people of God. He is a tireless advocate for the dignity of the human person, the sanctity of life and the care of the poor and vulnerable.

He is the successor to the Apostles in this local Church, entrusted with the ministry of teaching, sanctifying and governing the People of God.

“The Church must be a home for all, a place where every person can encounter the mercy and love of God.”

Sacred Art & Design

Cathedral & Architecture

The Cathedral is a testament to the faith of generations — a house of God built with reverence and artistry.

The Cathedral

The main worship space, designed to draw the faithful into the mystery of the liturgy through light, space and sacred art.

The Altar

The high altar, the centre of the Cathedral, where the Holy Sacrifice of the Mass is offered daily for the people of God.

Stained Glass

The stained-glass windows tell the story of salvation, casting colourful light upon the sanctuary and inspiring prayer.

The Shrine

The Shrine is a place of pilgrimage and quiet prayer, dedicated to the Sacred Heart of Jesus and the Blessed Virgin Mary.

Historical Features

The Cathedral preserves many historical features that connect the present generation with the faith of those who came before.

Gallery

A growing collection of historic photographs and images documenting the life, liturgy and development of the Cathedral.
